# ConservMisr

Welcome to the public profile for **ConservMisr**, a company dedicated to safeguarding Egypt's natural and cultural heritage through data-driven conservation initiatives. This repository serves as the home for our public documentation, community guidelines, and a high-level overview of the tooling that empowers our team.

## Mission
- Preserve and restore endangered ecosystems and historical landmarks across Egypt.
- Empower local communities with actionable insights for sustainable stewardship.
- Build collaborative partnerships with researchers, NGOs, and government agencies.

## What We Do
- **Conservation Intelligence:** We fuse satellite imagery, environmental sensors, and historical archives to map ecological risk and cultural site degradation.
- **Community Enablement:** Training programs and workshops equip local guardians with the skills to monitor and protect their surroundings.
- **Policy Support:** Evidence-based reports inform governmental and international policy decisions for long-term resilience.

## Tooling Capabilities
While our proprietary platforms are not published here, the ConservMisr engineering team leverages a suite of internal tools designed to streamline conservation work:

- **Habitat Insight Engine** – Performs geospatial analysis, species distribution modeling, and predictive habitat resilience scoring.
- **Heritage Sentinel** – Monitors structural health and visitor impact on archaeological sites with sensor fusion and alerting capabilities.
- **FieldLink Mobile Suite** – Enables offline-first data collection, automated validation, and secure synchronization for field teams in remote locations.
- **Atlas Data Fabric** – Orchestrates data ingestion, quality checks, and lineage tracking to ensure every dataset can be trusted and audited.
- **Impact Dashboard** – Provides interactive storytelling, KPI tracking, and executive reporting to showcase conservation outcomes to stakeholders.

## Public Repositories
We maintain a growing collection of open-source projects and datasets:
- **conservmisr-open-data** – Curated environmental and heritage datasets released under permissive licenses.
- **conservmisr-toolkit** – Shared utilities for geospatial processing, ecological modeling, and data visualization.
- **community-guides** – Training materials and best practices for partner organizations and volunteers.
